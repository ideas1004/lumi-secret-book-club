01 — OUR BEGINNING

It started with
one little sister.
And a question.

Lumi FOUNDER & STUDENT LEAD

One sister loves books.
The other hasn’t found the joy in reading—yet.

Secret Book Club begins with that difference. Instead of saying “read more,” we want to understand what makes reading feel uninteresting in the first place.

What if you chose the book? Or read with a friend?
What if you could defend a character or rewrite the ending?

Lumi and the student team will explore these questions through small reading experiments, keeping an honest record of what works, what feels difficult, and how their thinking changes.

Before you step inside

Do I need to be fluent in English?+

This club isn’t an English test. We’ll announce the languages used for meetings and books when registration opens, and plan conversations that help participating students feel comfortable.

How will the meetings work?+

We’re preparing a monthly online program: a book reveal, a mid-month activity, a creative challenge, and a closing conversation. Meeting times and joining instructions will be included in the launch announcement.

Do I have to finish every book?+

No. Share your thoughts on what you’ve read so far. If a book isn’t for you, tell us why and try another one. Please give a spoiler warning before discussing an ending.

How will we keep the club welcoming?+

Respect different opinions and avoid unwanted private messages. Keep meeting links and personal information private. Photos, videos, and feedback won’t be posted without permission from participants and, where needed, their guardians. Age-appropriate guardian guidance and adult support will be prepared before launch.
